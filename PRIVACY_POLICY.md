# Nagomi Mobile Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**
**Developer: SKLabs™**

## 1. Overview

Nagomi Mobile is a productivity application providing Pomodoro timers, focus sessions, study planning, subject management and productivity statistics.

Your privacy is important to us. Nagomi Mobile is designed to work locally on your device without collecting or transmitting your personal information.

## 2. Data Collection

Nagomi Mobile does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share personal data.

The application:

* Does not require an account.
* Does not use advertising services.
* Does not use analytics or tracking services.
* Does not track your activity outside the application.
* Does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following information may be stored locally on your device:

* Timer settings and preferences
* Subjects and study tasks
* Study plans and task queues
* Focus and Pomodoro session history
* Productivity statistics
* Theme, language and alarm preferences
* Active timer and countdown state

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to SKLabs™ or third parties.

## 4. Permissions

Nagomi Mobile may request the following Android permissions:

* **Notifications:** Used to display active timer countdowns and alarm controls.
* **Alarms and reminders:** Used to trigger timer alarms at the selected time.
* **Foreground service:** Used to keep timer and alarm functions working when the application is in the background.
* **Vibration:** Used when vibration is enabled for timer alarms.
* **Wake lock:** Used when necessary to ensure that timer notifications and alarms operate correctly.

These permissions are used only for the application’s timer and alarm features.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored locally until you:

* Delete tasks, subjects or session history from within the application.
* Reset the application settings.
* Clear the application’s storage through Android settings.
* Uninstall Nagomi Mobile.

Uninstalling the application may permanently delete all locally stored data.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Nagomi Mobile does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking or cloud-storage services.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

Nagomi Mobile does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. All application data is stored locally on the user’s device.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date shown at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
